About This
Site

Hey! We're two massive Rockstar Games fans who got tired of hunting across the internet for promo merch photos. So we built RockstarPromoHub — a single, searchable home for every piece of Rockstar promotional merchandise we can track down.

Whether it's a snapback handed out at E3, a Red Dead plushie from a launch event, or a GTA hoodie from an online store — if Rockstar made it, we want it catalogued here.

☁ Cloud Sync

This lets your catalogue save to the cloud so it works across all your devices and survives cleared browsers. It uses JSONBin.io (free, no credit card needed).

Steps:
1. Go to jsonbin.io and create a free account.
2. Click + Create Bin, paste {} as the content, click Save.
3. Copy the Bin ID (in the URL: /b/XXXXXX) and your Master Key (in Account → API Keys).
4. Paste them below and click Save. Done — all changes sync automatically.

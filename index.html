About this site

RockstarPromoHub started with a simple frustration: frustration searching the internet for accurate and detailed information about Rockstar Games promotional merchandise. So as two long-time Rockstar fans we decided to build the resource we wanted for this purpose pulling detailed information and photos about all Rockstar Games promotional items into one place.

From snapbacks handed out at trade shows to Red Dead plushies, launch-event exclusives, and GTA hoodies, we're always looking for more items to add. There's plenty of interesting material out there, and we're certain we've got a lot left to find.

What we cover

The aim is a catalogue of promotional merchandise from across the Rockstar Games universe, covering everything from older releases to the latest titles. Each item records the game it's linked to, its category, a description, release year, rarity, and availability where known.

How to help us

Found a piece of merch that's missing from the catalogue, have more information on an existing entry or spotted an error — we want to hear from you!

Head to the Contact page and let us know. Good-quality photos, source links, release dates, and any other useful information all help build a more complete catalogue.

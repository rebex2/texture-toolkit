Rockstar Merchandise Generator

Step 1 — Load current catalog.js

This tool automatically fetches the latest catalog.js directly from your GitHub repository when opened. You can also click "Fetch Latest" or paste catalog.js contents manually.
Tip: If auto-fetch succeeds, you don't need to touch Step 1 at all! Just fill in Step 2 below to add your item.

Step 2 — New item details

Fill in the details of the new merchandise item to add to the catalog.
